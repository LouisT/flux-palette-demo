Choose a preset, follow your system appearance, or build a custom palette from an accent color. Your selection stays with you as you explore the site.

<!-- more -->

## Configure the defaults

Set `sidebar.palette_selector.default_dark` and `default_light` in your site's `_config.flux-palette.yml`. An optional `include` list limits the published palettes; include both defaults.

## Make it yours

Try a few palettes on this article. Your selection is stored in this browser and persists as you navigate.
